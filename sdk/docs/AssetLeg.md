**assetSwapStructure** | **String** | The relationship between the funding leg&#39;s notional and the asset&#39;s principal, for a bond-family Asset.  Par (and absent) requires the funding leg&#39;s notional to equal the asset&#39;s principal; Proceeds allows the  funding leg&#39;s notional to be the market proceeds of the asset instead, sizing the two legs on independent  bases. Optional and absent by default; absence is distinct from Par so that behaviour keyed to the field  being supplied fires only when it is.     Supported string (enumeration) values are: [Par, Proceeds]. | [optional] [default to String]
**incomePolicy** | **String** | Controls what happens to income (coupons, dividends) generated by the Asset.  Defaults to PassThrough, preserving today&#39;s behaviour of emitting income as flows on their natural payment dates.     Supported string (enumeration) values are: [PassThrough, AccrueToReset, Reinvest]. | [optional] [default to String]
**initialPrice** | **java.math.BigDecimal** | The reference price of a single unit of the Asset at inception, used as the starting point (P_0) for  price-return resets. Optional and absent by default, preserving today&#39;s behaviour for TRS definitions  that predate price-return semantics. | [optional] [default to java.math.BigDecimal]
**payReceive** | **String** | Either Pay or Receive stating direction of the asset in the swap.     Supported string (enumeration) values are: [Pay, Receive]. | [default to String]
